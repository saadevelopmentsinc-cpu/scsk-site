Construction checklist FAQ

Common questions

Does SCSK include construction checklists?

Yes. SCSK includes built-in construction checklists and supports reusable checklist workflows for recurring site tasks.

Can checklists be used for safety and quality checks?

SCSK's checklist system is designed for recurring construction workflows, including safety, quality and completion processes where a consistent sequence of checks is useful.

Can I use checklists offline?

SCSK is designed offline-first so core field work can continue when internet access is unavailable or unreliable, then synchronise when connectivity returns.

What else does SCSK manage?

SCSK also covers projects, jobs, site diaries, site photos, team workflows, inventory, trade tools and professional project reporting.
