Construction inspection app FAQ

Common questions

Can SCSK be used for construction site inspections?

Yes. SCSK combines project and job records with site photos, checklists, notes, site diary workflows and reporting, making it suitable for documenting recurring site inspections and project checks.

Does SCSK work offline?

Yes. SCSK is designed offline-first so core field work can continue when reception is weak or unavailable, with synchronisation when connectivity returns.

Which devices are supported?

SCSK supports iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows. Larger screens are particularly useful for reviewing photos, job lists and reports.

Can inspection information be included in reports?

SCSK is designed to turn structured project activity, field evidence and records into professional project reporting.
