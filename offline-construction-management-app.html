Offline construction app FAQ

Common questions

Does SCSK work without internet?

Yes. SCSK is designed offline-first so core field work can continue when internet access is poor or unavailable, then synchronise when connectivity returns.

Why does offline construction software matter?

Construction sites often have inconsistent connectivity. Offline capability lets teams record work at the point it happens instead of delaying documentation or relying on a constant connection.

Can I use the same SCSK project on different device types?

SCSK supports iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows, with cloud synchronisation designed to keep authorised devices aligned when connectivity is available.

What can I manage in SCSK?

SCSK brings together projects, jobs, site diary activity, photos, checklists, inventory, team workflows, trade tools and project reporting.
