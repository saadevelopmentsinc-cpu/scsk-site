Site diary FAQ

Common questions

What is a digital construction site diary?

A digital site diary is an organised daily record of construction activity. In SCSK, that record sits alongside jobs, photos, checklists and project information so the day's activity remains connected to the wider project.

Can I use SCSK when there is no internet on site?

Yes. SCSK is designed offline-first so core field activity can continue through poor or unavailable reception, then synchronise when connectivity returns.

Can site photos support the daily record?

Yes. SCSK includes project-linked photo documentation, allowing visual evidence to stay alongside the project workflow rather than in a separate camera roll.

Can I use the site diary on a tablet?

Yes. SCSK supports iPad and Android tablets as well as phones and Windows. Larger screens are especially useful for reviewing records and producing reports.
