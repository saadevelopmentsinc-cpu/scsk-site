Defect management FAQ

Common questions

Can SCSK be used to track construction defects?

Yes. SCSK's project, job, photo, note, checklist and reporting workflows can be used to organise defects and the work required to address them within the broader project record.

Can I attach photos to site issues?

SCSK includes site photo documentation so visual evidence can be kept with project and job information rather than left in an unstructured camera roll.

Can SCSK be used on site without internet?

Yes. The app is designed offline-first so core field activity can continue through unreliable reception and synchronise when connectivity returns.

Is SCSK only for defect management?

No. SCSK also covers projects, jobs, site diaries, checklists, inventory, team workflows, photo documentation, trade tools and project reporting.
