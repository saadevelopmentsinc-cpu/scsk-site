Straight answers

Before you bring SCSK on site.

Does SCSK work without internet?

Yes. SCSK is designed offline-first, so core field work can continue when reception is poor. Changes synchronise when connectivity returns.

Can I use it in the office as well?

Yes. SCSK is available for Android and Windows, giving teams a field-ready mobile experience and a larger desktop workspace.

What can I manage inside SCSK?

Projects, jobs, teams, site photos, checklists, inventory, trade calculations and professional project records are brought into one connected system.

Is there a plan for different business sizes?

Yes. Starter, Solo Pro and Team options let you begin with the capability that fits your operation and move up as your needs grow.
